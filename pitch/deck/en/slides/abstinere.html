04 · ABSTENTION

When unsure, the agent does not guess

Input checks

  • the net load is missing
  • the last safe limit is missing
  • the newest reading is over 60 minutes old
  • a reading is absurd (over 2× installed power)
  • it is night

Uncertainty

spread = q90 − q05 is above the threshold τ

τ is chosen on validation: the highest threshold with at most 2% violations.

Is either one true?

YES · abstain

  • fixed limit of 36%
  • the case enters the review queue
  • the reasons go to the log

NO · confident

  • limit = q05 × kWp
  • confidence = 1 − spread / (2 × threshold)

Source: src/ml/abstention.py, src/agent/planner.py. The 2% target is a choice the team still has to confirm.

09

The theme’s requirement: the agent must be able to say “I am not sure” and send the case to a person. There are two kinds of reasons. First, input checks: missing, too old or impossible data, or night. Second, the model’s uncertainty: the spread q90 minus q05 above a threshold τ. The threshold is not picked by feel: we choose it on the validation period only, as the highest value with at most 2% violations. If no threshold meets the target, τ is minus infinity and the agent always abstains; we do not relax it quietly. When it abstains it applies today’s fixed limit, so it is never worse than the current situation. The crew has a budget of 20 cases a day and the screen shows when it is exceeded.
