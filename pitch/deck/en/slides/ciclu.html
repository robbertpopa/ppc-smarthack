Every plan moves through clear states: proposed, approved, executed, or rejected. A plan blocked by the guard or cancelled by the kill switch can be proposed again; a plan rejected by a person stays rejected. Execution first needs approval and is refused while the kill switch is on. The log is an append-only JSONL file with seven event types. It is also the agent’s memory: at start-up the service rebuilds its plans, today’s review count and the kill switch state from the log. An event is written before the state it describes, so a failed write leaves the state unchanged. Every decision can be traced from the log back to the forecast that produced it.
