04

The chart is illustrative. The bars are the energy a house could export during a day, hour by hour. The dashed line is the fixed limit, 36% of installed power. The stepped line is the dynamic limit. At midday there are hours when the grid has room and the fixed limit cuts energy for nothing (teal). Around noon, when many houses export at once, even the dynamic limit cannot let everything through (orange). In the evening the safe limit drops below 36%: there the agent protects the grid. There is no measured number on this slide.
