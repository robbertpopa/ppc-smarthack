The model predicts, one hour ahead, the highest export limit that will be safe, as a share of installed power. The inputs are the 14 columns in metadata.json: net load now and three earlier readings, yesterday at the same hour, the last safe limit, the sun’s elevation and the calendar. We use quantile regression (gradient boosting from scikit-learn) with five quantiles. The planner uses q05, the cautious quantile, because errors do not cost the same. The spread, q90 minus q05, is the uncertainty measure that abstention relies on. The chart values are the contract example, not a real forecast. Why a model and not only physics: physics needs the load and the sun of that moment, while the operator needs the limit in advance.
