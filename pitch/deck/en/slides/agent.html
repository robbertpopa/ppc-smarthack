05

There are not five agents, there is one with four stages, exactly what the theme asks for: perceive, estimate, decide, act. Perceive: the station’s net load, time of day, sun position, last safe limit. Estimate: our quantile model. Decide: the planner picks a limit per house and may abstain. Act: the guard checks, a person approves, the plan is executed and logged. Underneath is the digital twin of the grid, which is not ML: it produces the training labels and also checks every plan before execution. The LLM is optional and only rewrites explanations from numbers.
