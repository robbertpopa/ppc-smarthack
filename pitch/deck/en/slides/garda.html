Why a guard if the model is already cautious? Because the guard does not depend on the model. It takes the plan and the readings, recomputes the whole station with the plan’s limits using its own power flow, and rejects the plan if voltage, lines or the transformer go out of bounds, if the batteries make no sense or if a limit exceeds the contract cap. It fails closed: no forecast means no pass, and any exception blocks the plan. We say it honestly: this is a simulation check, not a certified protection system. The guard assumes a balanced three-phase grid and does not check phase imbalance. The 1.05 pu threshold comes from a press summary and is not verified at the connection point.
