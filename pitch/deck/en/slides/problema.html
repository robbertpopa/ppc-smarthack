02

The problem in three numbers. Romania has over 330,000 prosumers and overvoltage cases have already appeared, for example 253.40 V in Botoșani, above the level at which inverters trip and production is lost. Poland is the closest precedent: 614 incidents in 78 days in one network. Australia already has flexible limits, but operators run them. The confidence level of every source is in docs/06; AFEER and APCE have commercial interests and we treat them with caution.
