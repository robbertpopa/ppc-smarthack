08

Separation in time is the basic rule. We train from July 2010 to March 2012, validate in April to June 2012 and keep one full year, July 2012 to June 2013, sealed for the final evaluation. The consumption and generation data is Ausgrid, 300 homes in Sydney, licence CC BY 3.0 AU. The labels are synthetic: our power flow computes them and we compared them with OpenDSS. Every record has an available_at field so we never use information from the future. Caveat: validation covers only three months of autumn and early winter, which is why we saw calibration drift on the test year; model v2 proposes validation across all seasons.
