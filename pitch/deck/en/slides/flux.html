02 · THE IDEA

One decision, in seven steps

1 Forecast

q05, q90 and spread

2 Checks

missing, stale or absurd data

3 Sure?

spread under threshold

4 YES

limit = q05 × kWp, capped by contract

4 NO

fixed limit 36%, case for a person

5 Guard

power flow on the whole station

6 Person

approves or rejects

7 Execute

limits + action log

Not sure sends the case to a person. The crew has a budget of 20 cases a day.

Guard blocked: the plan cannot be approved and guard_blocked is written to the log.

Kill switch: cancels open plans and stops execution.

Source: src/agent/service.py, src/agent/planner.py. With SOURCE=model the real model; otherwise a mock forecast.

This is the main diagram. Step 1: the model gives a forecast with quantiles; q05 is the cautious value and spread is q90 minus q05. Step 2: we check the inputs for missing, stale or absurd data. Step 3: the central question, is the model sure? If yes, the limit is q05 times the house’s installed power, capped by the contract. If not, we apply the fixed 36% limit and the case goes to a person. Step 5: the guard recomputes the whole station with its own power flow. Step 6: a person approves or rejects, which is mandatory for unsure cases. Step 7: execution and the log. Below: what happens when unsure, when the guard blocks, and the kill switch.
