What we do not know

1

It is a simulation

Synthetic or German grid, Australian profiles. Not PPC’s network.

2

Optimistic observability

The model sees uncensored load. In a real system, curtailed production is invisible.

3

Ideal inverters

Reactive-power control assumes every inverter responds. In Romania that is not guaranteed.

4

The rules

We do not know whether ANRE rules and contracts allow variable limits per prosumer.

5

The 1.05 pu limit

It comes from a press summary. The exact rule at the connection point is unverified.

6

Simplifications

Balanced three-phase, no fairness analysis between houses, transformer capped at 100%.

The guard is a simulation check, not a certified protection system.

We say openly what we do not know, because the jury will ask. It is a simulation, not PPC’s network. The model sees uncensored load, and in a real system curtailed production is invisible. Reactive-power control assumes ideal inverters. We do not know whether ANRE rules and contracts allow variable limits per prosumer; that is the first question for the operator. The 1.05 pu threshold comes from a press summary. The grid is balanced three-phase, we did not analyse fairness between houses, and the 100% transformer limit is conservative: a thermal model would tolerate short overloads.
