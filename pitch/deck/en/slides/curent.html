03

The current starts at the panels, passes through the inverter, then through the house, which consumes first. What is left reaches the meter, the delimitation point. The 9 kW limit is measured at the meter and programmed into the inverter, but the number comes from the grid. The physical bottleneck can be in three places: the service connection (maximum current), the low-voltage line (heating and voltage rise) or the transformer station. Remember: kW is power, kWh is energy.
