Three scenes and a bonus, in five minutes

We say it first: this is a model of one station, not PPC’s network, and nothing controls a real inverter.

Scene 1 · 10:00 UTC

A normal day

  • propose a plan: green houses
  • the guard passed
  • approve, execute, log

Scene 2 · 05:30 UTC

Uncertainty rises

  • the forecast band widens
  • houses turn yellow
  • back to the fixed 36% limit

Scene 3 · the queue

A person decides

  • the case waits in “Not sure”
  • review budget: 0 of 20
  • reject, then the kill switch

Bonus · 20 kVA trafo

The guard blocks

  • we start with a weak transformer
  • plan blocked: loading over 100%
  • physics says no

Approve, execute and open the log. Scene 2, 05:30 UTC: the band widens, the spread passes the threshold, houses turn yellow and the limit falls back to 36%. Say: when the model is unsure, it does not guess. Scene 3: the case from scene 2 waits in the “Not sure” queue; show the review budget, reject, and optionally turn the kill switch on. Bonus: restart the API with TRANSFORMER_KVA=20; the plan is blocked with the reason “Transformer loading exceeds 100%”.
