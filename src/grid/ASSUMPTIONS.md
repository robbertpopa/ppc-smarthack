# Grid assumptions and limitations

- The prototype uses a synthetic balanced three-phase radial grid, not SimBench or the PPC network. It contains a 20 kV source, one 400 kVA transformer, and three feeders, with 70 houses per feeder and ten houses per electrical node by default.
- An optional SimBench benchmark (`topology: simbench`) uses `1-LV-rural3--0-sw`, preserving its transformer and topology while replacing existing loads and generators with input homes distributed over existing load buses. It is a separate benchmark and is not the three-feeder synthetic case or a PPC network. Transformer capacity comes from SimBench, not `transformer_kva`.
- SimBench's transformer has a 150-degree phase shift. A flat angle initialization previously converged to an implausible near-zero voltage or failed to converge. The twin now uses DC initialization with voltage angles enabled, and the no-injection case and a 210-house test are regression tested. The guard also checks the voltage range. Solver convergence alone is not evidence of a safe or physically meaningful result.
- Each feeder has seven 30 m segments, with resistance 0.206 ohm/km, reactance 0.08 ohm/km, and a 300 A rating. These are illustrative assumptions, not measurements.
- Active power only: load and PV reactive power are set to zero. PV export is curtailed at the meter after local consumption; all inverters are assumed to follow the limit perfectly.
- LV voltage defaults to a range of 0.90–1.05 pu; transformer and line limits are 100%. These voltage thresholds are scenario parameters, not claims about verified regulatory limits.
- The available 2012–2013 Ausgrid generation and load values are energy per 30-minute interval; multiply by two to obtain average kW. The data uses Sydney local time and is converted to UTC for labels. All selected households have PV.
- The label generator keeps local intervals beginning from 06:00 until before 20:00 as a simple daylight window. This fixed window is a simulation assumption, not astronomical daylight. It excludes households missing any controlled-load row for a selected day if that household has controlled-load records in the dataset.
- In the PV stress scenario, both PV production and installed panel capacity are multiplied by four, while consumption is unchanged. These are synthetic scenario values, not measured production.
- The safe-share search scans 21 points and refines the uppermost feasible boundary. It can miss narrow feasible islands between scan points; no feasible scan point yields null, which must not be interpreted as zero.
- When supplied, each house's `contract_cap_kw` caps its planned limit and its contribution to the safe-share calculation. The guard rejects plans exceeding that ceiling. Minimum voltage remains an internal safety check because the current shared `GridState` schema does not expose it.
- Battery defaults are 10 kWh, 1–10 kWh state of charge, 5 kW maximum charge/discharge, and 90% round-trip efficiency. Positive battery power means charging. This sign convention must be agreed with P1 before integration. The guard tests a prospective battery action; it does not persist state or execute commands.
- The guard assumes PV can be curtailed as commanded. It is a simulation-only prototype, not a safety-certified protection system. Its checks do not include three-phase imbalance.
- The first day used for the preview has only safe-share 1.0 labels. It is not by itself a useful training set and must not be represented as evidence of a gain from the agent.
- During a search any non-converged power flow produces a null label. Null may also mean no feasible scan point was found; these cases require further investigation and must not be used as numerical targets.
